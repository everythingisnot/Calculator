    How to play

    1

    Write a word in the quill bar and press Write. The object appears next to Wren. Each word costs ink, and each isle has a limited supply.

    2

    Add adjectives in front of the noun: giant, tiny, long, metal, flaming, frozen, friendly, strong, a colour and more. They change size, material and behaviour, and most cost 1–2 extra ink.

    3

    Drag objects onto things in the world: a ladder onto a ledge, a plank across a gap, honey onto a bear, water onto a fire. Drop gear such as wings, a torch or scuba gear onto Wren to equip it.

    4

    Tap the ground to walk there. Drag empty space to turn the camera, and pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap any object to see its stats: reach, length, weight and power.

    5

    Watch for side effects. Fire spreads to wood and cloth. Angry animals chase you. Goats eat wooden things. Lava burns wooden bridges. You have three hearts per isle.

    ★

    Stars: one for reaching the Lumen, one for staying at or under par ink, and one for finishing without losing a heart.
